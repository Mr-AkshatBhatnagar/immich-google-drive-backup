Privacy Policy

Immich Google Drive Backup is a personal application used by its owner to back up a self-hosted Immich photo library to the owner's Google Drive account.

Data handling

This application is for personal use and does not collect, sell, or share personal information with third parties.

Files are transferred directly between the owner's computer and the owner's Google Drive using rclone.

Google Drive access

The application accesses Google Drive solely to perform the owner's backup operations. OAuth credentials are stored locally on the owner's computer.
